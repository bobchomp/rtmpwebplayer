How It Works

This site is a self-hosted live streaming platform. Here's the whole process, start to finish.

  1. 1. Create a channel

    Each channel is one broadcast source - typically one camera or one service. Give it a name under Channels and it generates an RTMP URL and a private Stream key.

  2. 2. Connect your streaming software

    Open your channel's page and copy the RTMP URL and Stream key into your encoder (OBS, vMix, or similar) under its stream settings. Keep the stream key private - anyone with it could broadcast as you.

  3. 3. Go live

    Start streaming from your software. Within about 15 seconds the channel shows as LIVE on its dashboard page, with a live preview and a viewer count.

  4. 4. Share it

    Copy the embed code from the channel page and paste it into your own website - the video plays right there, with no account needed to watch. If you'd rather share a direct link instead (WhatsApp, email, social media), add a Public Link output and copy that link.

  5. 5. Optional: send it elsewhere too

    Click + Add output on a channel to relay the same live stream to another destination at the same time - a custom RTMP address (Restream, Facebook, etc.), or connect a YouTube account to relay there automatically. Each output has its own on/off switch, so you can turn any of them off without affecting the others or the live embed itself.

  6. 6. Optional: record it

    Turn on Record streams on a channel before you go live, and the broadcast is saved to private cloud storage automatically. It takes 5–30 minutes to appear after the stream ends while it's processed - find it under Recordings, where you can play it back, download it, or delete it. Recordings are never public.

  7. 7. See who watched

    The Stats page shows anonymised viewer analytics - roughly how many people watched, when, and their approximate location - for internal reporting only. It's never shown to viewers.

  8. 8. When you stop

    Stop streaming from your software (or just close it) and the channel automatically flips back to offline within a few seconds - the embed shows an offline state, any relays stop, and a recording (if it was on) finalizes and starts uploading.
