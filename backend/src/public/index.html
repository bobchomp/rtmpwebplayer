Live streaming on your own website

Send your stream from OBS or any other streaming software, and RTMP Web Player shows it in a player on your own website. Viewers don't need an account, and you don't need a third-party streaming service.

What it does

A player for your website

Paste the player into any web page. Visitors watch there, without leaving the site or signing up.

Recording

Record a stream to watch back or download later. Recordings are private to the organisation.

Viewer stats

How many people watched and roughly where from, for the organisation's own use.

YouTube and Facebook

Send the same stream to YouTube and a Facebook Page at the same time, with one title set in one place.
