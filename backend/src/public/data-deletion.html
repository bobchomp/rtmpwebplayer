Deleting your data

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Administrators can connect a Facebook Page or YouTube channel to stream.rossmackenzie.co.uk ("the Site") so their live streams are published there. This page explains how to remove what the Site keeps about that connection.

What we store

  • For Facebook, an access credential for the Facebook Page you chose, and the Page's name and ID.
  • For YouTube, an access credential for your YouTube channel, the channel's name and ID, and the details of the live stream created on it.
  • For both, the title, date and view counts of each broadcast the Site created, shown on your Stats page.

Viewers who watch through the Site's player never sign in with Facebook or Google, so we hold no Facebook or Google data about them.

How to delete it

  1. In the dashboard, open the channel, find the Facebook or YouTube output under Outputs and click Delete. The stored credential and connection details are deleted straight away, and deleting the whole channel does the same for all its outputs. To remove the view counts too, select those rows on the Stats page and delete them.
  2. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or "Apps and websites"), find RTMP Web Player and remove it. This stops the credential working straight away.
  3. On Google, go to your Google Account's third-party access settings and remove RTMP Web Player.
  4. Or email info@rossmackenzie.co.uk. We'll delete everything we hold about your connection within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

Live videos already published on your Facebook Page or YouTube channel are yours and stay there. Delete them on Facebook or YouTube if you no longer want them. See our privacy policy for more.
