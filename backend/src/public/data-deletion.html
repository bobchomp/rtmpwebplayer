Deleting your data

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Administrators can connect a Facebook Page or YouTube channel to stream.rossmackenzie.co.uk ("the Site") so their live streams are published there. Here is how to remove what the Site stores about that connection.

What we store

  • Facebook: an access credential for the Facebook Page you chose, and the Page's name and ID.
  • YouTube: an access credential for your YouTube channel, the channel's name and ID, and the details of the live stream created on it.

Viewers who watch through the Site's player never sign in with Facebook or Google, so we hold no Facebook or Google data about them.

How to delete it

  1. From the dashboard: open the channel, find the Facebook or YouTube output under Outputs and click Delete. The stored credential and connection details are deleted immediately. Deleting the whole channel does the same for all its outputs.
  2. From Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or "Apps and websites"), find RTMP Web Player and remove it. This stops the credential working straight away.
  3. From Google: go to your Google Account's third-party access settings and remove RTMP Web Player.
  4. By email: write to info@rossmackenzie.co.uk and we will delete everything we hold about your connection within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

Live videos and broadcasts already published on your Facebook Page or YouTube channel are yours and stay there - delete them on Facebook or YouTube if you no longer want them. See our privacy policy for more.
