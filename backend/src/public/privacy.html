Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This policy explains how stream.rossmackenzie.co.uk ("the Site") handles information. The Site is used to broadcast live services and events, and lets administrators manage and record those broadcasts.

Who this applies to

There are two kinds of people who use the Site:

  • Viewers - anyone watching a live stream or recording through the embedded player or a public watch link. No account or sign-up is needed to watch.
  • Administrators - a small number of people we authorise to manage channels, streams, and recordings via a password-protected dashboard.

Information we collect from viewers

When you watch a stream, our server automatically logs, for internal analytics only:

  • Your IP address, used to estimate a general location (e.g. country/city) via an offline lookup database - your IP address is never sent to a third party for this purpose.
  • Basic technical details such as when playback started and stopped, and roughly how many people are watching at once.

We do not require or collect names, email addresses, or any other personal account information from viewers. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties, other than as described under "Who we share information with" below.

Information we collect from administrators

  • A username and password to access the management dashboard.
  • If an administrator chooses to relay a stream to YouTube or Facebook, we store the access credentials (OAuth tokens) that Google or Facebook issue to us, along with basic channel/page details, so we can publish the stream on the administrator's behalf. These credentials are stored securely on our server and are never exposed to visitors' browsers.

Recordings

Administrators can optionally turn on recording for a channel. When enabled, the stream is recorded and stored in private cloud storage, accessible only to administrators for playback or download. Recordings are automatically deleted after the retention period the administrator has configured, or can be deleted manually at any time.

Who we share information with

  • Google (YouTube Data API) - only when an administrator chooses to relay a stream to YouTube, so the stream can be published there.
  • Meta (Facebook Graph API) - only when an administrator chooses to relay a stream to Facebook, so the stream can be published there.
  • Cloudflare - our hosting/CDN provider, and (if recording is enabled) our storage provider for recorded video files.

We do not sell personal information to anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information obtained through the YouTube Data API is used solely to let an administrator publish their own live stream to their own YouTube channel, and is not used for any other purpose.

Data retention

  • Viewer analytics (approximate location, watch timestamps) are retained indefinitely for internal reporting unless an administrator deletes them.
  • Recordings are retained for the period an administrator configures, then automatically deleted, or deleted sooner on request.
  • OAuth credentials are retained until an administrator disconnects the relevant YouTube/Facebook integration.

Security

Access credentials and other secrets are stored only on our server and are never sent to or stored in visitors' browsers. The management dashboard is password-protected and restricted to authorised administrators.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the anonymous viewing analytics described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
