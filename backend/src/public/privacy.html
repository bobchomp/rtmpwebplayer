Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how stream.rossmackenzie.co.uk ("the Site") handles information. The Site broadcasts live services and events, and lets administrators manage and record those broadcasts.

Who this applies to

Two kinds of people use the Site:

  • Viewers: anyone watching a live stream through the embedded player or a public watch link. Watching needs no account or sign-up.
  • Administrators: the small number of people we allow to manage channels, streams and recordings, through a dashboard that requires signing in.

Information we collect from viewers

When you watch a stream, our server automatically logs, for internal analytics only:

  • Your IP address, which we use to estimate your general location (such as your country and city) with a lookup database kept on our own server. Your IP address is never sent to anyone else for this.
  • Basic technical details, such as whether you're on a phone or a computer, when you started and stopped watching, and roughly how many people are watching at once.

We don't ask viewers for names, email addresses or any other account details. We don't use this data for advertising, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone, except as described under "Who we share information with" below.

Information we collect from administrators

  • Administrators sign in through our login provider, Auth0, which confirms who they are and tells us their email address. We use that address only to check they're authorised, and to keep each administrator's channels and data separate from everyone else's. We never see or store their password.
  • If an administrator chooses to connect their Restream account, we store an access credential that lets us read the view counts of their past streams. We use it for nothing else, and they can disconnect it at any time.
  • If an administrator chooses to connect a YouTube channel, we store an access credential issued by Google and the name of that YouTube channel. See "How we use Google user data" below.
  • If an administrator chooses to connect a Facebook Page, we store an access credential issued by Facebook for that Page, and the Page's name and ID. We use it only to create a live video on that Page each time they go live, set and update its title and description, end it when they stop, and read how many people watched it, for the administrator's own stats page. We don't read or change anything else on the Page or their Facebook account. See Deleting your data for how to remove it.

How we use Google user data

When an administrator connects a YouTube channel, we ask Google for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly permissions on their Google Account, and use them only to:

  • Read the name of their YouTube channel, to show which channel is connected.
  • Create a reusable live stream on that channel, and a new live broadcast each time they go live, with the title, description and privacy setting chosen in our dashboard.
  • Update that broadcast's title and description if the administrator changes them while live, and set its thumbnail to the image they chose in our dashboard.
  • Read how many people are watching those broadcasts while they're live, and afterwards how many views they've had in total and by country, for the administrator's own stats page.

We don't read, change or delete their existing videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or anything else on their YouTube channel. We don't share this data with anyone else, and nobody reads or reviews it. Our server only uses it automatically, for the actions above.

The credential Google issues is stored only on our server and is never sent to any browser, including the administrator's own. It is deleted when the administrator removes the YouTube output from our dashboard, and they can also revoke it at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings.

This feature uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel, administrators agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of their data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

We don't set cookies or store anything in viewers' browsers. Administrators get a single cookie that keeps them signed in to the dashboard, and the dashboard remembers a small display preference in their browser.

Recordings

Administrators can turn on recording for a channel. The stream is then recorded and kept in private cloud storage, where only administrators can play or download it. Recordings are deleted automatically after the period the administrator has set, and administrators can delete them sooner at any time.

Who we share information with

  • Cloudflare, which delivers the Site to visitors and, if recording is on, stores recorded video.
  • Auth0, which handles administrator sign-in. Viewers never deal with it.
  • Restream, only for administrators who connect their Restream account, and only to read their own streams' view counts. Nothing about viewers is sent to Restream.
  • Google (YouTube), only for administrators who connect a YouTube channel, to publish their streams there. Nothing about viewers is sent to Google.
  • Meta (Facebook), only for administrators who connect a Facebook Page, to publish their streams there. Nothing about viewers is sent to Meta.

We don't sell personal information to anyone.

Data retention

  • Viewer details (IP address, approximate location, device type and watch times) are kept for the organisation's own reporting until an administrator deletes them.
  • Recordings are kept for the period an administrator sets, then deleted automatically. They can be deleted sooner on request.
  • An administrator's sign-in lasts up to 7 days. A connected Restream, YouTube or Facebook credential is kept until the administrator disconnects it (see Deleting your data).

Security

Access credentials and other secrets are kept only on our server and are never sent to visitors' browsers. Only authorised administrators can sign in to the dashboard.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will appear on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
